# A shared daily reset and clearer adventures

September 26, 2026 · Live in [Cosmic Court](https://cosmic.shillex.us)

## What changed

- **Daily reset at 3 PM Mountain / 5 PM Eastern.** Get 30 turns together each day. Unused turns carry over up to 90, including missed days offline. Gift turns remain separate and never expire. The schedule follows daylight saving time.
- **Galactic Senate opens at level 3.** Find it near the top of Adventure and in the footer. Existing favor and titles are preserved.
- **Latest combat results appear above your moves.** See what just happened before choosing your next ability. Earlier rounds are expandable for easier phone play.
- **Review before selling.** Single-item sales now confirm the item and payout. Bulk sales already have a review step, and equipped gear stays protected.
- **Auto ×5 warns when auto-heal is off.** Cancel to enable it in Recovery options, or continue. Auto-heal pays the medbay below 60% health when you have enough credits.
- **Discovery reports tell the right story.** Failed investigations show the actual discovery hazard, recorded damage, and remaining health. They no longer name an unrelated enemy. Leaving a discovery is distinguished from a setback.

## Two rules clarified

Both sides act in the same combat round. A killing blow does not cancel the enemy's action. If it also knocks you out, you are defeated and need recovery. Your equipment is safe.

Assigned attributes are a commitment. Choose your first specialization free at level 3; later specialization changes or redistribution cost credits and become more expensive with repeated resets. Newly earned points and ability loadout changes remain free.

## Your progress carries forward

Characters, equipment rolls, accounts, favor, and history are preserved. Existing whole turns earned under the old timer were settled during the changeover. There is no extra deployment gift.
